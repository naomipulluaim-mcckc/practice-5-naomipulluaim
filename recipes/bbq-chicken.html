Dinner · serves 4 · 40 minutes

Sticky BBQ Chicken

One tray, one sauce, and a forty minute window where you do almost nothing. Start it before you set the table.

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken thighs, skin on
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 200 ml barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Spring onions, to finish
