Dinner · serves 4 · 40 minutes

Sticky BBQ Chicken

One tray, one sauce, and a forty minute window where you do almost nothing. Start it before you set the table.

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken thighs, skin on
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 200 ml barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Spring onions, to finish

Method

  1. Heat the oven
  2. Season the chicken
  3. Mix the sauce
  4. Roast, then glaze
  5. Rest and serve
1

Heat the oven

220°C, fan on. Put the empty tray in while it heats. A hot tray is the difference between crisp skin and steamed skin.

Back to the steps

2

Season the chicken

Pat the thighs dry, then rub with oil, paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Dry skin browns. Wet skin does not.

Back to the steps

3

Mix the sauce

Stir the barbecue sauce, honey and vinegar together. Taste it. It should be sweeter than you want, because the oven takes the edge off.

Back to the steps

4

Roast, then glaze

Roast skin side up for 25 minutes. Brush on half the sauce, roast 10 more. Brush on the rest, roast 5 more. Three coats, not one.

Back to the steps

5

Rest and serve

Five minutes on the tray before it moves. Scatter the spring onions. Serve with the macaroni salad if you made it yesterday.

Cool the rest and keep it for tomorrow, or pick another recipe
